# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Delivery Leads

### Role Summary
Delivery Leads coordinate day-to-day execution across the team so planned work moves smoothly from commitment to completion. They work closely with PMs, developers, and QA to keep delivery moving and remove blockers quickly.

### Responsibilities
- Track execution against the plan and surface risks early
- Coordinate daily priorities, dependencies, and handoffs
- Help resolve delivery blockers with developers and QA
- Confirm work is progressing toward committed milestones
- Escalate schedule or scope concerns to the Project Manager

### Goals
- Keep the team focused on near-term delivery outcomes
- Reduce surprises during execution
- Improve flow of work from planning through release

### Typical Communication
- Daily check-ins with PM, developers, and QA
- Status updates on dependencies and blockers
- Coordination notes for timeline changes or scope trade-offs

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Works with the Project Manager to manage execution details and reporting
- Partners with Product Managers to understand priority and scope changes
- Supports developers by clarifying sequencing and resolving blockers
- Coordinates with QA to ensure readiness criteria are met before release

---

## QA Leads

### Role Summary
QA Leads own test strategy, acceptance readiness, and defect triage. They provide quality oversight so the team understands what must be validated before features can be accepted or released.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain the test approach for the initiative
- Confirm acceptance criteria are testable and complete
- Coordinate test execution and defect triage with QA/testing and developers
- Track quality risks and communicate readiness concerns
- Validate that fixes and regressions are covered appropriately

### Goals
- Increase confidence in product quality and release readiness
- Prevent escaped defects through early quality checks
- Make testing responsibilities clear across the team

### Typical Communication
- Test planning sessions and bug triage meetings
- Readiness updates with PM, Delivery Lead, and developers
- Quality summaries for stakeholders when needed

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to clarify acceptance criteria and edge cases
- Works with Project Managers and Delivery Leads to align testing timing with delivery milestones
- Partners with developers on defect investigation and fix verification
- Coordinates with QA/testing contributors to distribute coverage and execution work

---

## Stakeholder Liaisons

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Liaisons consolidate stakeholder input, updates, and approvals so communication stays clear and actionable. They help ensure feedback is captured consistently and routed to the right decision-makers.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and organize stakeholder feedback and requests
- Prepare concise updates for stakeholder review
- Track approval needs and decision points
- Ensure questions, concerns, and dependencies are routed appropriately
- Help avoid conflicting or duplicated stakeholder guidance

### Goals
- Improve clarity in stakeholder communication
- Reduce missed approvals and misaligned expectations
- Create a single point of coordination for updates and feedback

### Typical Communication
- Regular stakeholder update meetings or written summaries
- Approval and decision tracking
- Feedback consolidation with PM and PdM

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to translate stakeholder input into product decisions
- Works with Project Managers to align communication timing and status reporting
- Coordinates with Delivery Leads when stakeholder decisions affect schedule or scope
- Shares relevant updates with developers and QA when feedback changes implementation or test plans

---

## Release Coordinators

### Role Summary
Release Coordinators manage release readiness, coordination, and post-release verification. They help make sure the team is prepared to deploy, communicate, and validate changes after release.

### Responsibilities
- Track release requirements, milestones, and go/no-go items
- Coordinate release tasks across developers, QA, PM, and support
- Verify readiness of notes, approvals, and deployment steps
- Coordinate post-release checks and issue follow-up
- Capture release outcomes and any follow-up actions

### Goals
- Make releases predictable and well-coordinated
- Reduce release-day confusion and missed steps
- Ensure post-release verification happens consistently

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews
- Go/no-go checkpoints
- Post-release status updates and verification notes

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers to keep release timelines and communications aligned
- Works with developers to confirm deployment steps and fixes are complete
- Coordinates with QA/testing to ensure validation is done before and after release
- Partners with PM/PdM and support on release messaging and follow-up actions
